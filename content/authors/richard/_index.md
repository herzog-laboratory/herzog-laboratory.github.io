Richard Essink (MSc) is a research assistant working on establishing epigenetic editing in hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs).  The aim of this research line is bidirectional; 1. establishing whether ageing related DNA methylation are a causative driver in HSC ageing and 2. whether reversion of these changes through epigenetic editing can rejuvenate HSCs. He has a deep background in HSC biology, having worked on lineage differentiation assays in Dr. Adam Wilkinson’s Lab at CSCI, as well having run a CRISPR-KO screen for regulators of HSC emergence in Dr Emile van de Akker’s Lab at Sanquin Research. He combines this with expertise in epigenetic editing, acquired working on endocrine resistance in Prof. Dr. Pernette Verschure’s Lab at Swammerdam Institute of Life Sciences. He completed his BSc at Amsterdam University College, continuing with his MSc at University of Amsterdam, for which he was awarded the Amsterdam Science Talent Scholarship.
