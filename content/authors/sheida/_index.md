Sheida Goosheh (DVM/MSc) is a Research Assistant at the Stem Cell Institute, University of Cambridge. Her research focuses on tissue ageing across multiple organs. The aim of Sheida’s research is to understand DNA methylation signatures associated with ageing across the human body and identifying surrogates that best reflect systemic ageing.

Sheida previously worked as a Research Assistant in the Haematology Department in Professor Anthony Green’s lab, where she gained experience in clonal haematopoiesis and ageing. She subsequently worked in Dr Jyoti Nangalia’s lab, contributing to research into somatic mutagenesis and clonal evolution in haematological diseases and cancers.

**If you had 10 extra healthy years in life, what would you do more of?**

I would hope that advances in ageing and rejuvenation research would mean not just living longer, but having more healthy years to enjoy. I would love to spend those years travelling, trying food from all over the world and learning to dance salsa properly.
