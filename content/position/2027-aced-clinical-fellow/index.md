Our lab is open to hosting Clinical Research Training Fellows through the 2027 Intake of the International [Alliance for Cancer Early Detection (ACED)](https://www.earlycancer.cam.ac.uk/aced-cambridge/aced-funding) Programme.

ACED is open to talented and motivated clinical doctors with a strong academic background and passionate about research. Interested candidates should submit their application directly through the ACED Portal:

[Further information & submit your expression of interest here.](https://www.earlycancer.cam.ac.uk/aced-clinical-research-training-fellowship-2027)

Expressions of interest must be submitted by **12 October, 2026**. Successful students will then seek their principal supervisor for a full proposal due 11 January 2027. 

*Please note this is a competitive funding opportunity for individual applicants.*
